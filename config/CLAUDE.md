# Chess config

The scene is `SceneConfig.xml` plus the node files of `config/nodes/` (the engine's "Node files", `engine/src/Modules/BECore/Scene/CLAUDE.md`); what the nodes do is in `src/Chess/Components/CLAUDE.md`, read it before changing the scene. An entry that changes a node edits only the file of that node. The settings files (`ChessSettings.xml`, the git-ignored `ChessSettings.local.xml`) are in `src/Chess/Engine/CLAUDE.md`, "The settings file".

| File | Holds |
|------|-------|
| `SceneConfig.xml` | the `scene` element (`ChessScene`), `viewVariants` (the default view 840 x 800, `Tall` 720 x 1204 up to the aspect 0.59, `Portrait` 720 x 960 and `Wide` 1120 x 800 from the aspect 1.4) with its comment, and the 49 `<node ref>` in the draw and input order (the order of the references decides it for one layer, so do not reorder): the seventeen UI nodes, then the 32 piece references with their group comments |
| `nodes/ChessBoard.xml`, `ChessBoardCoordinates.xml`, `ChessMaterial.xml`, `ChessMovePanel.xml`, `ChessHud.xml`, `ChessEditorBar.xml`, `ChessPuzzleBar.xml`, `ChessMenuPanel.xml`, `ChessOpponentPanel.xml`, `ChessMovesSection.xml`, `ChessGameSection.xml`, `ChessSoundPanel.xml`, `ChessPositionSection.xml`, `ChessPuzzleSection.xml`, `ChessPromotionPanel.xml`, `ChessResult.xml`, `ChessSound.xml` | the nodes `Board`, `BoardCoordinates`, `Material`, `MovePanel`, `Hud`, `EditorBar`, `PuzzleBar`, `MenuPanel`, `OpponentPanel`, `MovesSection`, `GameSection`, `SoundPanel`, `PositionSection`, `PuzzleSection`, `PromotionPanel`, `Result`, `Sound` (the node name has no prefix; the layer is the node's own) |
| `UIThemes.xml` | the colors of the board as theme tokens `board*` (the two squares and the nine marks), read through `BoardPalette`, and the coordinate labels' `textColor`; the themes `tavern` (the default: cream and dark oak, chosen for the beer pieces), `classic` (the look the board had before the themes, held by `BoardPaletteTest`) and `slate`; the `ChessUiTest` fixture selects `classic`. **A theme defines every token** (`ExpectThemesComplete`). **A new theme** is a `<theme>` here and needs no test change: `BoardPaletteTest` runs on every theme of the file and fails a mark that cannot be seen or told from another over either square, or a label that does not read on its cell (the thresholds are named there, set to what `classic` meets) |
| `nodes/ChessPiece.xml` | the prefab of a piece; each of the 32 references overrides `name`, `spriteId`, `file` and `rank` (spelled out even when equal to the prefab's) |
| `TextureLibrary.xml` | the 12 piece sprites (`WhiteKing` ... `BlackPawn`, the names of `PieceSpriteId`) as the atlas group `pieces` in `assets/atlases/pieces.png`; the entries are written by the Texture editor's Pack, by Accept of a member in the Generate panel (it repacks the group) or by the disabled `ChessPieceAtlasTest.DISABLED_RewriteTheShippedAtlas` (`tests/CLAUDE.md`), only the header comment is edited by hand. The sources stay in `assets/artgen/` (one PNG each, from `artgen/presets/chess_piece.json`, with its `.artgen.json` beside it) and stay out of the web package; `ChessPieceArtFilesTest` and the holding test of `ChessPieceAtlasTest` keep the files and the library together |

A node file is named `Chess<Node>` because `ConfigManager` loads it as a config by its stem. A new file in `config/nodes/` needs no entry for the web package (`tests/check_web_package.cmake` reads the tree), but the scene must reference it (`ChessSceneFilesTest` fails on a file nobody references). A test reads the scene through `tests/ShippedScene.h`, which expands the references, never with `load_file`: the nodes are not in the scene file.

**The bars** (`ChessEditorBar.xml`, `ChessPuzzleBar.xml`) are non-modal canvases of layer 20 that their component shows. The editor bar is a wrapping stack of 56-unit buttons in the bottom margin where `Opening` / `Status` stand (two rows in Portrait and Tall, at the right of the board in Wide, where a home bar leaves 48 units under it). The puzzle bar is one row of three buttons at the bottom left, where `Opening` stands (empty while a puzzle is on), at x 80 and at x 12 in Portrait and Tall. **Known limits** (each bar's matrix test allows exactly this): the editor bar, in a window with side insets that gets the default view (the scene 840 units wide), covers 20 units of the board's lowest rank, because the home bar lifts the safe edge to y 768; the puzzle bar does the same in such a window in the default view and in Wide.

**Node anatomy** (the behaviour is in `src/Chess/Components/CLAUDE.md`):

- `Material`: two groups mirrored about the board's middle (in Portrait two rows, above and below the board), `MaterialTop*` and `MaterialBottom*`, each a balance label and five rows (icon + count `{material<Top|Bottom><Type>}`); in the move panel a second set `MaterialPanel*` (horizontal lists of 52-unit chips and a balance) over the player bands.
- `MovePanel`: five bands 6 units apart, `MovePanelBand<Top|Bottom>Player`, the list of 7 rows of 56 units `MovePanelRow<i>`, `MovePanelBandButtons` (the step buttons `<prefix>StepBack`, `StepForward`, `Latest`, 56 x 56 at its left, and Take back) and `MovePanelBandAnalysis`. The rows are two columns, of four and three (`MovePanelColumn0/1`), one under the other in Wide; the Tall variant (`maxAspect:0.6`) puts the box under the board and the lower material row, hides the two player bands (the material keeps its Portrait rows), sets the columns side by side and swaps `MovePanelBandAnalysis` for the twins `MovePanelTallHint` / `MovePanelTallEval` in the buttons band. `MovesSection` has the same rows.
- **Known limits:** moving a material group breaks the mirror (the owner's call): a "+39" beside a rank "8" may read as one number; a top notch lets `Turn` cover the upper group (Portrait 3:4), and in the panel `Menu` and `Reset` cover its right. The HUD follows the safe area, the move panel's box does not: a notch pushes `Menu` and `Reset` onto the top band, and the home bar of side insets lifts `Status`, so in Wide a variant of `ChessHud.xml` makes it 38 units high: never over the box.
- `PromotionPanel`: `PromoteQueen` .. `PromoteKnight` (`ChessPromote`) and `PromotionCancel` / `PromotionDim` (`ChessCancelPromotion`).
- `OpponentPanel`: `Dim`, `Plate`, `ModeGroup` (`opponentMode`, `ChessSetMode`), `ColorGroup` (`opponentColor`, `ChessSetColor`), `EngineGroup` (`ChessSetEngine`), `LevelGroup` (`ChessSetLevel`), the `OpponentError` line and `OpponentBack`. A segment needs an explicit `height`.

**In the browser** the engine's web overlay (`engine/web/config/`: `ApplicationConfig.xml`, `LoggerConfig.xml`, `SDLWindowConfig.xml`) replaces a file of the same name **whole**; Chess has no web config of its own. Its `ApplicationConfig.xml` must stay the overlay's apart from `renderer` (`ChessWebConfigTest` fails otherwise): an attribute added here, such as a title or a `mode`, would be dropped in the browser. Do not give `config/` a file named like an overlay file. The files of `config/` and `assets/` that stay out of the web package (`*.md` such as this one, `*.local.xml`, `imgui.ini`, `.DS_Store`, `*.py`, `__pycache__`, the piece sources `assets/artgen/*.png`) are in `cmake/ChessWeb.cmake`; `tests/check_web_package.cmake` reads the tree (no list to edit) and holds that every sprite of `TextureLibrary.xml` is in the package; it runs in ctest and after every web link.
